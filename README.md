ABOUT THE GAME

Chronicles of the Martial World is a browser-based, text-driven Murim RPG inspired by classic martial arts novels and the world of Jianghu.

Begin as an ordinary martial artist and gradually build your reputation, master martial arts, cultivate internal energy, explore the Central Plains, join powerful factions, and write your own martial legend.

Your decisions influence your journey, relationships, reputation, and place in the martial world.

The game is designed to be playable on desktop computers, tablets, and mobile devices.

CORE FEATURES

CULTIVATION SYSTEM

- Murim-inspired cultivation progression.
- Meditation and internal energy development.
- Knowledge accumulation.
- Realm breakthroughs.
- Breakthrough requirements and progression resets.
- Manuals that influence cultivation efficiency.
- Pills and elixirs for special progression requirements.

MARTIAL ARTS AND MANUALS

- Discover martial arts manuals.
- Different manual qualities and tiers.
- Manual learning minigames.

COMBAT SYSTEM

- Sword and fist-based attacks.
- Defensive stances and evasive actions.
- Ki techniques and energy attacks.
- Different Murim enemies.
- Weapon mastery progression.

WEAPONS AND INVENTORY

- Pixel-art inventory icons.
- Toggleable item descriptions and statistics.
- Multiple weapon types.
- Equipment progression.
- Sword collection and upgrades.

HOW TO PLAY

1. Open the live game link.
2. Complete the beginner tutorial.
3. Begin cultivating and accumulating knowledge.
4. Discover martial arts manuals and equipment.
5. Train your character and improve your combat abilities.
6. Explore the martial world and meet NPCs.
7. Build your reputation.
8. Join a faction when you reach the required progression.
9. Continue your journey toward the highest martial realms.

3. Open index.html in a modern web browser.
